Omni-Directional Rover  
Tiny robot, running on an ESP32 and 3D-printed Mecanum wheels (which were a nightmare to put together). Controllable wirelessly through a webserver.  Please ignore the Li-ion vice deathtrap we used as a power-supply. Built for Microbots Hackathon 2024.  
ESP32, C++, CAD
