PathMaxer  
Tour guide robot prototype. Users upload a schedule from a smartphone app, and the robot guides them between their classes. Uses Dijkstra's algorithm for pathfinding, runs on an RPi 4, and uses ultrasonic sensors to hopefully not hit anything.  
RobotOS, Raspberry Pi, React Native, C++
