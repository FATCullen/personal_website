UTRA Hacks Robot  
Small robot, designed and programed to solve the UTRA Hacks 2025 "capture the flag" style challenges.  Lots of pathfinding issues, almost no sleep, and an incredibly limited sensor array, but our team pulled through.  We ended up with 2nd place overall.  
Arduino, C++
