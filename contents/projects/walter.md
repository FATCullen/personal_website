WalTer Bot  
Navigates and captures images from a room full of electronic components, then categorizes them via image processing.  Provides an LLM interface to help you plan hardware projects with the found parts.  It also has a cute little OLED face!  
Arduino, Raspberry Pi, Next JS, Google Vision, Google Gemini
